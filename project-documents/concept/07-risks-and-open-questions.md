# Risks and Open Questions

Status: Draft

## Product risks

- Is the target user and moment specific enough?
- Does the environment supply a real advantage?
- Is the intervention useful at the moment it appears?

## Technical risks

- Which integration has the longest uncertain setup path?
- Which dependency could prevent an end-to-end demo?
- What fallback preserves the central claim?

## Agent risks

- Where could the agent infer more than the evidence supports?
- What uncertainty must be visible?
- Which actions require human review or approval?

## Demo risks

- What depends on network, credentials, external services, timing, or live data?
- Can the demo be reset quickly?
- What can be recorded or seeded without misrepresenting functionality?
