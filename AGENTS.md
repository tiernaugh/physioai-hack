# Project Instructions

## Purpose

This repository supports a hackathon project for **Agents, Everywhere: Bots, Channels, & More**. Help the team ideate, choose, scope, build, test, and pitch one strong embedded-agent experience.

## Product principles

- The agent must live where people already work, talk, or live.
- The environment must provide context, triggers, relationships, or actions that materially change the agent's behaviour.
- Reject concepts that are essentially standalone chatbots or generic retrieval systems in a different wrapper.
- Prefer one reliable, memorable end-to-end workflow over broad scope.
- Treat human visibility, steering, approval, and decision authority as core interaction design.
- Map sponsor technology to a chosen behaviour; do not let available technology generate the concept by itself.
- Design for a roughly three-hour effective build and a clear two-minute demo.

## Working practices

- Read `project-documents/01-hackathon-brief.md` before evaluating major product choices.
- Keep raw source material separate from interpretation and decisions.
- Record material product or technical decisions in `project-documents/05-decisions-log.md`.
- Put exploratory alternatives in `project-documents/03-idea-exploration.md`.
- Once a concept is selected, maintain its source of truth in `project-documents/concept/`.
- State assumptions and uncertainty explicitly.
- Critique weak ideas candidly and score concepts consistently against the judging criteria.
- Protect working code and unrelated teammate changes. Avoid broad rewrites during the build window.

## Definition of success

The project should answer all four questions convincingly:

1. Does it work?
2. Does it belong in this environment?
3. Is it technically real?
4. Is it actually useful?
