- Dataset: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
- Source geometry: `isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip`, BodyParts3D 4.0
- Source application: https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas (MIT)

Adaptations inherited from Human Atlas: axes and units converted from millimetres/Z-up to metres/Y-up; geometry simplified with meshoptimizer; normals quantized to signed 16-bit; curated system metadata. This project further extracts and repacks the muscular layer into a single compressed browser asset.

This adult male reference anatomy is an educational visualization. It is not a diagnostic model, a scan of an individual, or a complete representation of human anatomical variation.
